A century on the cliff edge

The Azure Grand opened in 1924 as a handful of rooms for sailors waiting out the meltemi. Today it is 180 rooms and suites, three restaurants and a pool that ends where the sea begins.

What hasn't changed is the idea that a guest should never have to wait for help. Ring the concierge from your room, your phone or this page, and your request goes straight to the team who will bring it.

During your stay

Anything on this list is one call away. Tell the concierge your room number and what you need.

  • Housekeeping

    Room cleaning, fresh linen, turndown and an extra bed for the little ones.

  • In-room extras

    Towels, pillows, bottled water, toiletries and breakfast on the balcony.

  • Maintenance

    Air conditioning, hot water, the TV or anything else that isn't working as it should.

  • Spa and fitness

    Book the gym with a trainer or a treatment at the cliffside spa.
